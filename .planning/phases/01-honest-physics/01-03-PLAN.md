<objective>
Make photovoltaic generation reach primary energy and the efficiency grade by extracting the
existing, validated kWp-to-kWh arithmetic into the physics layer, feeding the renewable leg from
declared capacity, reporting the clipped surplus, and stating a zero capacity as an assumption.

Purpose: `calculatePrimaryEnergy` already nets renewable generation correctly — the only thing
missing is a non-zero value reaching it. The value exists: `calculateSolarPotential` computes it
for the economics path and `applyPhaseToMaterials` already writes a measured kWp back into
materials. The two have simply never been connected, so every PV measure in this product has
moved money and left the modeled grade untouched.

Output: `src/lib/energy/pv-generation.ts` with a named-parameter signature that closes the
sixth-positional-argument hazard, a renewable leg fed by declared capacity, a clipped-generation
field on `PrimaryEnergyBreakdown`, and a rewrite of every sentence that still asserts the old
zero.
</objective>
