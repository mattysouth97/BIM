irradiance — into one resolved `ClimateRegion` value produced once at the payload boundary, wire
it to every consumer that currently defaults to Seoul, regionalize the two weather fields D-20
names, and make an unresolvable region refuse rather than guess.

PHYS-02: shipping D-18's refusal without the wiring would delete every building's PV figure.

a regionalized `coolingSeasonSolar` and `summerDesignTemp`, a `ScenarioBuildingInputs` that
publishes the resolved region once, and nine deleted Seoul defaults.
