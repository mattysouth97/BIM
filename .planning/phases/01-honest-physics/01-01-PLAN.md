<objective>
Make a lighting change move the two numbers the product shows for energy intensity — the
efficiency grade's primary intensity AND the on-screen site intensity — by replacing the flat
share-of-total fuel split with four named, fuel-declaring end uses fed by a real lighting load.

Purpose: `deliveredFromDemand` is the gate. While it derives lighting as a share of the HVAC
total and fixes on-site generation at zero, no lighting or PV measure can move the modeled
grade however correct its own saving formula is. Research established a second, textually
unconnected gate: `calculateSystemBreakdown` owns an independent `SYSTEM_RATIOS` lighting term
and is what reaches the displayed site intensity. Fixing one and not the other ships the
failure mode this repository has already hit twice — two numbers for one building.

Output: two new pure modules (`lighting-load.ts`, `end-uses.ts`), a replaced
`deliveredFromDemand` signature, a `calculateSystemBreakdown` whose lighting term is the same
computation, an LPD provenance union carried from `material-inference.ts` to the screen, and
every call site and contract test moved onto the new shape.
</objective>
