<objective>
Converge the twin and diagnostics retrofit-economics paths onto one shared pure core so the same
building returns the same result whichever page computed it, and enforce that with a contract
test that fails the build on any divergence.

Purpose: the two paths arrive at different `wallU` derivations, diagnostics hand-derives a
residual heating figure instead of re-running the engine per measure, and — the gap research
found that reconciliation alone cannot close — diagnostics generates no photovoltaic measure at
all. Its measure-id set therefore cannot equal the twin's until one is added. D-15 defines the
same result as identical ids, identical kilowatt-hours, identical grade delta and identical
financials; nothing weaker is in scope.

Output: `src/lib/retrofit/retrofit-core.ts`, two thin adapters over it, a photovoltaic measure on
the diagnostics side sized from measured roof planes or from a named ratio assumption, and
`twin-diagnostics-parity.test.ts`.
</objective>
