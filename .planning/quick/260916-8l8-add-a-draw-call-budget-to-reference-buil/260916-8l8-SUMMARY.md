## Addendum — orchestrator verification, 2026-09-16 (`ae6805d`)

The orchestrator re-verified this task rather than accepting the execution
report, and found one real defect in the shipped artifact.

**All five waiver reasons inverted the mechanism they described.** Each read
"N distinct geometries collapse into M instanced shapes, placed P times across
E elements". Every integer was correct and sourced — the digit-traceability
assertion passed — but the relationship was backwards.

`drawCalls` is `nodes.length` in `scripts/lib/ifc-glb.mjs`, summed over two
`nodes.push` sites: one node per instanced shape, plus one merged batch node per
non-instanced group. So the M instanced shapes are the ones that **stayed
separate**, at one draw call each — which is exactly why the count is high — and
the low-repetition remainder is what collapses, merged into the few per-group
batches. Confirmed three independent ways:

- the two `nodes.push` sites in the generator and `drawCalls: nodes.length`;
- `drawCalls - instancedShapes` is a small constant on all five layers
  (6, 4, 5, 4, 5);
- the `groups` name lists — sixty5/plumbing carries `duct, valve, terminal,
  fitting, equipment, plant` (6 merged nodes) plus three `*-instanced` names
  covering the 2456 instanced nodes; 2456 + 6 = 2462.

Corrected in `ae6805d`. Reasons are now **generated from the manifest rows**
rather than hand-typed. No figure changed.

**The relevant limitation, now a comment on the test:** the digit-traceability
assertion passed both before and after the correction. It checks that every digit
is sourced; it cannot check that the sentence built from those digits is true. An
inverted relationship between correct numbers is invisible to it — only reading
the generator caught this.

This is the eleventh instance of the pattern AGENTS.md documents, and it was
found inside the guard written to catch that pattern.
