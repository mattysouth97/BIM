## The blocker in full

`GAP-1` is this repository's named defect class — a correct number with a false
explanation beside it — in its most consequential form yet, because the two numbers
disagree with each other.

`generateRetrofitMeasures` computes a package's saving by **rerunning the engine on
the selected package**, and says why in its own note: measures interact, so isolated
savings are not additive. Two surfaces disregard that and add the isolated figures
anyway — one by plain sum into the exported 보고서, one through a damping heuristic
into the twin's insights dock. Neither states its method. The outliner is the sharp
case: it already holds the authoritative `scenario` object and renders the damped
sum beside it.

So for a single selection this product can print two different annual savings, and
the one that reaches the customer-facing export uses the method the engine itself
documents as invalid for a package.

This was reported by a delegated integration checker and then **re-verified in this
session against the actual files** before being recorded here.
